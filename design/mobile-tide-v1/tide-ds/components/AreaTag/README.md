# AreaTag

A white pill with a coloured dot and a word that says which part of the app something belongs to, like the reference's "Personal" and "Work" tags.

**Use** on cards in the date rail, on detail headers, and under the now card. The dot uses the area token: `area-readings`, `area-medicines` (medicines, refills, lab), `area-visits`, `area-team`, `area-me`.

**Values** height 30, `radius-pill`, `surface` with `shadow-card`, 14/500 `ink`, 8px dot. On a navy band the pill becomes translucent white with `on-band` text.

**Don't** fill the pill with the area colour; use red, amber or green dots; drop the word.
