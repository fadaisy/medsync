# StatusPill

The state of a reading or a request, round, always with its icon and word; its colours are owned by the Clinical Safety Officer and carried unchanged from MedSync.

**Use** under a reading ("In your range", "Above your target", "Danger") and on requests that wait on the clinic ("Waiting for the clinic"). The target in the words comes from the patient's own clinician-set target.

**Values** height 30, `radius-pill`, 14/600, 15px icon. Icons are fixed: check (stable), circle-dot (caution), triangle-alert (danger), clock (pending).

**Don't** use these colours for anything that is not about health; drop the word; invent new states without CSO sign-off.
