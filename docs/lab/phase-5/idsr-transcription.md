## Annex 2G: case-based laboratory reporting form (printed p. 124)

**Part I (referring health worker):** date of specimen collection; suspected disease or condition; specimen type
(blood, plasma, serum, aspirate, CSF, pus, saliva, biopsy, stool, urethral or vaginal discharge, urine, sputum,
food or water); specimen unique identifier (same as the case-based form's patient identifier); patient name(s);
sex; age (years, months, days); date the specimen was sent to the lab; clinician's phone and email.

**Part II (laboratory):** laboratory name and location; date received; specimen condition (adequate or not);
type of test(s) performed; final result(s); date results sent to the district; date sent to the clinician; date
the district received them.

## Corrections to the prompt's H17 and the research notes

- The weekly list is **HMIS 033b**, not the 23-item list in the UNIPH bulletin. RR-TB is a weekly row (**DR**);
  "laboratory-confirmed MDR and XDR-TB" as worded in the bulletin does not appear.
- Table 2 has 32 entries. It includes Hepatitis B, Typhoid, Bacterial meningitis and Listeriosis, which the
  research's IDSR summary did not list as immediate.
- Malaria appears on HMIS 033b weekly (MA) and in Table 3 monthly, not in Table 2.
