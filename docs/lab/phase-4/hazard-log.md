**Steps 9, 10 and 11 are conditional.** STOP 0 found none of their inputs in hand (no partner interface document,

**Who sets the scores.** Severity and likelihood are proposals for the CSO and the laboratory lead to set. They are
not a signed assessment. No clinical number, code, turnaround or range is proposed anywhere in this log.

## Key

The Phase 3 log carried no scores. This log uses the 5×5 scheme of the Pharmacy Phase 1 log, with the meanings
written for the lab.

**Severity (S)**

| S   | Word         | Meaning                                                                                                         |
| --- | ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Negligible   | No harm; a record is untidy and is put right                                                                    |
| 2   | Minor        | A result is briefly late, or a record is wrong and caught before anyone acts on it; no harm expected            |
| 3   | Moderate     | A result is late, missed or misread with possible temporary harm; or one patient's privacy is lost              |
| 4   | Major        | A wrong result is acted on, a result sits on the wrong patient, or many patients' privacy is lost; harm likely  |
| 5   | Catastrophic | Death or permanent harm likely, including staff or the public exposed to a high-consequence infectious specimen |

**Likelihood (L)**, per live lab site

| L   | Word           | Meaning                  |
| --- | -------------- | ------------------------ |
| 1   | Very unlikely  | Not expected in years    |
| 2   | Unlikely       | About once a year        |
| 3   | Possible       | About once a month       |
| 4   | Likely         | About once a week        |
| 5   | Almost certain | Daily, or already so now |

**Risk = S × L.** 1–4 low · 5–9 medium · 10–16 high · 20–25 very high. "Before" is today's paper practice with the
Phase 3 baseline. "After" assumes the control is built, its test passes, and the signatures this slice names are in
force. For a conditional step, "after" is the risk **if** the step is built; until then the risk is today's paper
practice, unchanged.

**Test words.** `unit` = Vitest without a database, under `tests/lab/phase-4/`. `lane` = the PGlite lane,
`tests/emr-db/lab-phase4-*.test.ts`, run with `EMR_PGLITE=1` (one connection, so races are proven one transaction
after another). `harness` = the local screenshot harness with axe-core (no Playwright; STOP 0 Q15). `owner` = a run
or trial only the owner or site staff can do. `.test.ts` is dropped from file names. `S1`–`S51` are the scenarios of
§9 of the prompt.

**Status words.** Every hazard is `open — closed by test at Step N`: N is the step whose STOP must show the named
test passing. A conditional hazard says so and names the gap report that stands in for it.
