**To:** The Commissioner, National Health Laboratory and Diagnostic Services, Ministry of Health
**Copy:** Division of Health Information
**Subject:** Request for interface specifications and onboarding: IRRDS, ResTrack, A-LIS and the National
Laboratory Data Repository

{Hospital name}, {HMIS facility code}, uses MedSync for its clinical and laboratory records. We refer specimens
through the national sample transport network and wish to:

1. create requests and receive results through IRRDS, and receive sample-tracking events from ResTrack, so that
   results return to the requesting clinician without retyping;
2. where a partner facility's laboratory runs A-LIS, exchange orders and results with it;
3. submit laboratory data to the National Laboratory Data Repository as the Ministry requires.

We request: the interface specifications and data standards for each system; access to a test environment; the
onboarding and certification steps; the data-sharing agreement template and the signatory; and a technical
contact.

We will exchange only the fields the specification requires, under the Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019. Our
registration with the Personal Data Protection Office is {number}.

{Name, title, contact}
