are the research's own decisions. The LD decisions record what the owner and laboratory lead agree; they sign no range.

**One choice differs from the recommendation: LD-05.** Viral load is read with the WHO 2023 categories (undetectable;
suppressed when detected and at or below 1000 copies/mL; unsuppressed above 1000). The Uganda MoH 2022 rule treats
1000 itself as non-suppressed, so a result of exactly 1000 is "suppressed" in MedSync and "non-suppressed" under the
national rule. Show the category's source with every result.
