# Lab Phase 2: user guide (one page per role)

Draft wording, for the CSO to check before go-live. Everything below appears only where the hospital's flag is on.

## The doctor

- **Ordering.** Each test says what the lab can do today: in the lab here (hours, usual turnaround), will be referred,
  analyser down, or not known today. A test the hospital does not offer has no Add. The same line shows in the order
  sheet, the Plan and the resus hand-over.
- **Results.** A result flagged on a decision scheme shows its category in words (for example "↓ Moderate anaemia")
  and the line saying which scheme flagged it; the lab's reference interval is shown for information. "Pregnancy not
  recorded" or "Altitude not adjusted" appear when they apply. eGFR always carries its caveat.
- **Bedside results** carry the Bedside tag, the device, the lot and who measured; a glucometer reading is never a
  diagnosis.
- **High-risk warning.** Flag "Suspected high-risk infection" with a reason; clear it, with a reason, when it no longer
  applies. Only clinicians can clear it.

## The collector (nurse or clinician)

- Print the label, stick it on the tube, then scan or type it. A match shows in teal; another patient's label shows in
  amber and the tube cannot be marked collected: print a new label. Tick "Wristband checked".
- Awaiting payment: in inform mode give a reason to collect now; in hold mode the cashier records payment first.
  Emergencies are never held.
- An amber high-risk block means: follow the hospital's procedure before collecting.

## The cashier

- Lab payments lists orders awaiting payment. Record Paid (receipt number), Insured (insurer, authorisation) or Waived
  (reason, approver). Restricted tests are counted, never named. You see no results and no diagnosis.

## The technologist

- Scan a label to receive it. A second scan says when it was received and by whom. Undo within five minutes.
- New specimens appear by themselves; "Live updates paused" means the page refreshes every 15 seconds instead.
- The clinical question is on the worklist and the specimen page; "Show the working diagnosis" opens it, and each
  opening is recorded.
- A high-risk specimen is received only after you tick "Procedure followed".

## The laboratory lead

- **Research drafts**: import the research once; every band arrives unsigned with its source. Record each check, keep one
  source where two disagree, then sign. Nothing flags until you and the CSO have signed.
- **Ranges and cut-offs**: choose what flags each result (reference interval or a decision scheme), set each scheme's
  boundaries, sign.
- **Analyser profiles**, **Test menu**, **Lab settings** (elevation, eGFR, HIV algorithm, payment, the high-risk
  procedure, bedside devices, lots and who may record bedside tests).

## The NCD clinician

- Lab results fill the visit's lab fields by themselves, with where they came from; a filled value cannot be typed over
  (the lab amends it). With no result in the window, order it or record an outside result with its laboratory.
