# Tether: business model and price list (draft for the owner)

Status: research draft, 16 November 2026. Prices are proposals, not approved. Every number links to a source in section 9, shown as [S#]. A number with no source is marked **ASSUMPTION** and given a range. Money is in Uganda shillings (UGX). Exchange rate used for USD prices: about **UGX 4,090 per USD** (market rate 11 Nov 2026 [S40]; Bank of Uganda rate UGX 4,045 on 8 Oct 2026 [S40]).

How the research was done: web search only. Many provider sites (Africa's Talking, Rocket Health, Pesapal) could not be opened from this session, so some figures come from search summaries of those pages or from third-party sites. Confirm the starred items in section 8 in writing before signing anything.

## 1. Summary (one page)

**The model in one sentence.** Safety stays free for every patient. Patients, families, employers and insurers pay for _proactive work by the hospital's own care team_. Hospitals pay Tether a platform fee for the software that brings them bookings, payments and programme revenue.

**Who pays Tether**

1. **Patients (or a sponsor) pay for Plus.** Plus is Tether's own product. Plus Family is UGX 8,000 a month per household, Plus Care is UGX 10,000 a month per person, and both together are UGX 15,000.
2. **Patients (or a sponsor) pay for a care programme.** The money goes to the hospital through split settlement. Tether keeps **UGX 9,000 or 20% of each monthly fee after the collection fee, whichever is higher**, and the hospital keeps the rest to pay its team.
3. **Hospitals pay a platform fee** of UGX 500,000, 1,000,000 or 2,000,000 a month depending on size. The pilot is free for 6 months.
4. **Hospitals pay a booking fee** of UGX 500 for each visit booked and completed through Tether. Patients never pay a booking fee.

**Price list (per month; a year costs 10 months; a quarter is 5% off)**

| Product                                            | Team                                                          | Monthly | Yearly  |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- | ------- |
| Plus Family (household)                            | software only                                                 | 8,000   | 80,000  |
| Plus Care (one person)                             | software, SMS, pharmacy packing                               | 10,000  | 100,000 |
| Plus Family + Care                                 | as above                                                      | 15,000  | 150,000 |
| Blood pressure programme: nurse-led                | nurse, medical officer                                        | 25,000  | 250,000 |
| Diabetes programme: nurse and nutrition            | nurse, medical officer, nutritionist                          | 30,000  | 300,000 |
| Blood pressure and diabetes programme              | nurse, medical officer, physician, nutritionist               | 50,000  | 500,000 |
| Diabetes on insulin: physician-supported programme | nurse, medical officer, physician, nutritionist               | 55,000  | 550,000 |
| Heart failure programme                            | nurse, medical officer, physician, pharmacist                 | 70,000  | 700,000 |
| Low mood and anxiety programme                     | counsellor, medical officer, psychiatrist or physician review | 75,000  | 750,000 |

**The honest result.** One hospital never pays for the company. A mature hospital earns Tether about **UGX 3.9 million a month** after direct costs. Tether's overheads are UGX 20 to 60 million a month (**ASSUMPTION**), so Tether breaks even at about **12 to 16 mature hospitals**. With 20 hospitals signed over about 30 months, the model turns monthly-positive around **month 33**. It needs about **UGX 0.9 billion (about USD 220,000)** of funding or grants before then (section 5).

### 3.3 Price formula and results

**price = cost ÷ (1 − margin − collection fee)**

The collection fee is set at **3.5%**. Published or sample rates are: Flutterwave about 3% in its own sample (1.4% per a directory) [S13], Pesapal about 3% to 3.5% [S14], Yo! 3% [S15], DPO 2% to 3% [S16]. The target margin is 30% for programmes and 35% for Plus.

Physician review is 3 hours a week per full caseload, following the collaborative-care norm [S7].

**Plus costs.** Plus Family costs about **3,000** a month: hosting 600, support 500, SMS 300, USSD 100 and acquisition 1,500. The formula price at a 35% margin is 4,878. Plus Care costs about **5,750**: 30 SMS 1,050, pharmacy packing 2,000 (**ASSUMPTION** 1,000 to 3,000), support 800, hosting 300, acquisition 1,500 and USSD 100. Its formula price is 9,350. The owner judged UGX 3,000 to 7,000 too low, and cost agrees for Plus Care. The proposed 8,000 / 10,000 / 15,000 gives margins of 59%, 39% and 46%. That is fair for software and still below one saved round trip: a 5 km boda ride costs roughly UGX 3,000 to 4,000 each way at Uber's 2018 rates [S33], and a hypertension clinic visit costs patients about USD 5.70 [S31].

## 5. Unit economics and projection

**Per member per month, Tether side:**

- Blood pressure programme: Tether keeps 9,000, minus 4,900 direct cost, which leaves **4,100**.
- Heart failure programme: Tether keeps 13,510, which leaves **8,610**.
- Plus at an average price of 10,450: about **5,000** after fees and direct costs.

**Assumptions per hospital** (all **ASSUMPTION**; ranges in brackets):

- **Outpatient visits:** 3,000 a month (1,500 to 6,000).
- **Share booked through Tether:** 5% in month 1, rising to 30% by month 12 and 40% by month 22.
- **Programme enrolment:** starts in month 3, with 15 new members a month (8 to 30) and 7% monthly churn (4% to 10%). That levels at about 200 members, roughly 15% to 20% of a chronic base of about 1,200 patients.
- **Programme mix:** blood pressure 50%, diabetes 20%, both 15%, insulin 5%, heart failure 5%, mental health 5%.
- **Plus:** 30 new subscribers a month (15 to 60) with 10% churn (8% to 15%), levelling at about 300.
- **Platform fee:** medium tier (1M), free for 3 months for hospitals after the pilot.
- **Onboarding:** UGX 5M per hospital.

**Tether fixed costs** (**ASSUMPTION**): about 2 engineers, a part-time clinical safety lead, support, hospital success staff, infrastructure, legal, data protection and audit. These run at 20M a month for pilot-only, 25M to 35M for the 5-hospital case, and 30M, then 45M, then 60M for the 20-hospital case.

**A mature hospital (month 36) brings in:** revenue **6.44M a month** (platform 1.0M, bookings 0.6M, programmes 1.9M, Plus 2.94M) and contribution after direct costs **3.9M a month**.

**Break-even.** Mature hospitals needed = fixed cost ÷ 3.9M.

- At 45M fixed, about **12** mature hospitals are needed (10 if contribution reaches 6M, 20 if it is only 2.3M).
- At 60M fixed, about **15** (10 to 26).
- The 20-hospital plan turns monthly-positive in **month 33**.
- The cumulative cash need peaks at about **UGX 892M (about USD 218,000)**.

The pilot alone is a learning cost of about UGX 230M a year unless grants cover it.

**What moves the result most:** (1) Plus uptake, which is the biggest Tether line; (2) programme enrolment and churn; (3) how fast hospitals sign; (4) fixed team size. Sponsored seats add revenue on top of the base case. For example, an employer paying for 100 blood pressure seats adds about 0.4M a month to Tether contribution.

**What patients already spend.**

- **Clinic visits:** a hypertension clinic visit in Kampala and Wakiso cost patients a median of **USD 5.70** (2020 dollars), and nearly half spent more than 10% of income on hypertension care [S31].
- **Sugar testing:** test strips cost about UGX 25,000 a pack at one pharmacy (undated) [S32].
- **Household budgets:** average household spending is about UGX 566,666 a month and median cash earnings are UGX 200,000 [S29].
- **Mobile Money:** there are 36.7 million active Mobile Money users (Q1 2026) [S30].
- **Disease burden:** hypertension affects 22% to 34% of adults, and only 5% to 8% of those aware of it are controlled [S34].

**What this means for pricing.** A UGX 25,000 programme is 12.5% of median cash earnings. That is affordable for salaried urban patients and families paying for a relative, and too much for the poorest. Sponsors and NGO seats are how they are reached.

## 7. Risks and how the model handles them

| Risk                                    | How the model handles it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Affordability**                       | Safety is free. Weekly payments are offered. A family member or employer can pay. NGO seats are near cost. The blood pressure nurse-led programme costs a fifth of Rocket Health's old chronic plan, which may also have covered medicines and tests [S18b].                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Clinical benefit is real but modest** | Nurse-led task-shifting improves BP control in sub-Saharan Africa [S35]. Insurance plus nurse-led care beat insurance alone in a Ghana trial [S35b]. HEARTS control rates vary from 5% to 86% [S35c]. Promise engagement and measured control, not cures. Use the insurer holdback to share the risk.                                                                                                                                                        |
| **UMDPC advertising and telemedicine**  | Names describe the condition and the team, never "premium/gold/better". There are no outcome claims in adverts [S36b]. UMDPC requires telemedicine to be an adjunct to in-person care, with a face-to-face consultation within the previous 12 months [S36c]. Programmes therefore enrol **only patients seen at the hospital**, and the licensed hospital delivers the care. Visit fees stay the hospital's own. A GP cap of UGX 30,000 was proposed [S36]. |
| **Looking like insurance (IRA)**        | The Insurance Act 2017 requires an IRA licence for health membership organisations [S37]. Tether's fees buy **defined services** (check-ins, reviews, sessions), never payment for treatment, drugs, tests or admissions, and there is no pooling of risk. Insurers stay the risk-carriers. Get a written legal opinion before selling to employers.                                                                                                         |
| **Payments (NPS Act 2020)**             | Patients pay through a Bank of Uganda licensed aggregator [S13], [S38]. Visit and programme money settles to the **hospital's** account. Tether's share comes by split settlement at the aggregator, so Tether never holds hospital or patient money. Plus is Tether's own sale, so it needs Tether's own merchant account. Split settlement in UGX is documented for Flutterwave in general [S13c] but **not confirmed for Uganda**. Pesapal: not found.    |
| **Payment failures and churn**          | Retry once, then a grace week during which safety continues. Yearly plans cost 10 months to cut churn. Offer refunds pro-rata for unused months. Refund fees are unverified.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Data protection (DPPA 2019)**         | Register with the Personal Data Protection Office (about UGX 100,000 a year) [S39]. Get consent for each sponsor report. Insurers and employers see aggregate figures only, never an individual's readings.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Dependence on Plus**                  | Plus is about 46% of Tether's revenue in the model. If uptake halves, break-even moves to roughly 20 hospitals. Test the price in the pilot before scaling.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Staff capacity**                      | Nurse time is priced at 200 patients per nurse. Track minutes per member in the pilot and re-price if they run above 50.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

## 8. Decisions for the owner

1. **Who employs the care team?** Recommendation: **the hospital**. It is licensed and meets UMDPC's in-person link. Tether only takes the platform share. A Tether-employed team brings more revenue but makes Tether a health provider.
2. **Tether's programme share.** Recommendation: **UGX 9,000 or 20%, whichever is higher**. A flat 30% leaves hospitals only 13% on mental health.
3. **Plus prices.** Recommendation: **8,000 / 10,000 / 15,000**, with Plus Care free for programme members. Test 10,000 vs 12,000 for Plus Care in the pilot.
4. **Weekly plans.** Recommendation: **yes, at +12%**. No daily plans.
5. **Pilot hospital terms.** Recommendation: **free for 6 months, half price for 6 months**, in exchange for outcome data shared with consent and a reference.
6. **First aggregator.\*** Recommendation: **whichever confirms UGX split settlement and a written MoMo rate of 3% or less**. Flutterwave if it confirms, Pesapal otherwise. Get settlement time and refund fees in writing.
7. **Which programmes launch first.** Recommendation: **blood pressure nurse-led and diabetes nurse and nutrition** (internal medicine first, about 70% of the mix). Heart failure and mental health follow after CSO design. Pregnancy blood pressure comes last.
8. **Sponsor sales.** Recommendation: start with **one employer and one NGO in year 1**, and insurers in year 2 with a legal opinion on IRA.
9. **Funding.** Recommendation: plan for **about UGX 0.9 billion over 30 months**, or keep the team under UGX 20M a month until 5 hospitals have signed.

\* Starred items must be confirmed in writing before signing.
