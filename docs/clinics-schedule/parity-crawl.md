### C. Capabilities (⚠ = the new views would not obviously cover it)

- Clinic create, rename, department, unit, place, audience, confidential, archive/restore → Clinics editor Basics.
- Planned → live per clinic (blocked until the department is live), plus publish-now materialise → Get ready / History. ⚠ The "department must be live first" dependency.
- Weekly / every-N-weeks / nth-weekday / one-off days, valid from/until, runs-on-holidays, Stop this day → Sessions tab.
- ⚠ Booked vs walk-in split (capacity_booked; blank means 60% of total), interleave booked:walk-in, overbooking factor 1.00–2.00. Only on the Scheduling rule editor, not on the hub day form.
- ⚠ Session-level clinician assignment and the T-12h unstaffed banner (`scheduling/page.tsx:179`).
- ⚠ Session delay ("Late by N min", patients messaged at ≥30 min). Real value; the Week plan needs it.
- ⚠ Cancel a single session: dry-run count, reason, patients told, next equivalent day offered. Closures cover date ranges only.
- ⚠ Service-line capacity mode (session vs clinician slots); slot-mode lines point users to clinician schedules (`clinics-view.tsx:784`).
- ⚠ Time to care (days), continuity default, reason codes, Hospital default line, Active flag. Probably the Booking tab, but these are not named.
- Payment posture, fee, deposit → Fee tab. ⚠ Per-cadre fee overrides (service_line_fees).
- ⚠ Department booking policy: slot, buffer, bookable weekdays/hours, reminder plan, auto-confirm, allow virtual, notes. Department-scoped, not per clinic.
- OPD opening hours (capability opd_hours) → Get ready / Closures? ⚠ Not named.
- Public holidays (read-only, provisional Eid) and per-clinic "runs on holidays" → Closures.
- Closures at hospital/department/ward/clinic scope, preview, lift → Closures. ⚠ Ward-scope closures and ward closure/bed closure are out of scope but live in the same table.
- Places (building/floor/room/directions, archive) → ⚠ no Places view in the list; needed by the clinic Place picker.
- ⚠ Department CRUD, go-live per department, start department, heads (acting, until), units, dispensing points, level presets, legacy import. Should stay in the hub.
- ⚠ Availability problem log (equipment_down etc., expected back, confirm still down).
- ⚠ Wards, beds, layouts, in-charges, bed board. Out of scope.
- ⚠ Emergency capabilities, verify/reject (four-eyes, ADR 0158).
- People: clinician leave/unavailable blocks (availability_blocks) → People leave requests. ⚠ Today these are self-service by the doctor; there is no admin approval flow and no leave-request table.
- ⚠ Clinician settings: auto-confirm, slot, buffer, virtual, out-of-hours override.
- ⚠ Staff approvals, request info, suspend/deactivate/reactivate, MFA reset, role/department reassign, invitations send/revoke. Grade is shown read-only (`pending-detail-sheet.tsx:178`). No offices or consent controls exist anywhere in hospital-admin.
- ⚠ Go-live checklist: start department live, ≥1 ward, primary and backup admin, then sa-activate-hospital (which may return 403 for a non-superadmin, `actions.ts:178`). Also location, operating profile, contacts.
- Desks and screens: desk_devices (label, mine_department_ids) and desk_pins exist (migration 0294), but the only writes are set_pin/unlock and close_day/undo_close_day in `ha-reception-desk/index.ts:39-60`. **No admin UI or op creates or edits desk devices, and no pairing flow exists.** `/clinic/screen` is a signed-in staff page. The "pair" search hits are lab exchange (`app/api/exchange/pair`) and nurse assignments, both unrelated. Pairing/coverage would be net-new.
- History: hub panels have an item History tab (`itemHistory` → `loadItemHistory`, `data.ts:993`) that reads the audit trail. ⚠ No draft/publish/restore for clinics or schedules exists; the only draft/publish is ward layouts (`ops-wards.ts:38-53`). clinic_sessions.status has 'draft' in its CHECK (`clinic-sessions.ts:176`), but the materialiser writes published rows directly (`lib/scheduling/materialise-sessions.ts:29`).
- ⚠ Capacity readouts (places, held, unstaffed, 14 days), Sent today / SMS cost, Pending approvals list on Scheduling.
- ⚠ Print timetable.
