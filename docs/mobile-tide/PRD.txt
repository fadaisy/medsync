Summary
Tide v1 rebuilds the MedSync patient app (Expo) around one calm, near-white design
language, with 22 screens, a floating pill nav and motion on every screen. It also fills the
gaps the old app had: getting in, booking, notifications, Help, and a full dark mode.
What is new compared with the current app:
A new design system, Tide. Soft grey ground, navy ink, pill controls, a tonal card
cascade, Figtree and Geist Mono type, and a full light and dark theme.
A floating pill nav with a raised centre plus that opens quick actions in place.
Getting in: an animated splash, a sign-in with three methods (phone code, password,
health card scan), and a 4-step sign-up with a map pin.
Booking that works end to end: come today (live queue per doctor), ask for a day ,
payment, and real tear-off tickets.
Care: a redesigned medicines screen, typeable readings with sugar contexts, trends,
interpreted lab results, move and eat, and a care team chat.
Me: a flipping health card, people I care for , privacy , language and text size.
Safety: a Help · SOS emergency flow reachable from every screen, plus a dangerous-
reading interrupt.
Notifications with actions inside each notification.
The prototype is the design canvas "MedSync Mobile · Phase 1 Look and Feel" (pages "v2 ·
Tide" and "v2 · Tide dark"). The build prompt is claude/PROMPT-MOBILE-TIDE-V1.md.
Problem, goals and success metrics
The current patient app covers monitoring and adherence, but it feels busy and has no
real path in, out or across. Getting in, booking and safety are thin, and nothing tells the
patient what is happening next. P atients are often older , on low-end Android phones, with
intermittent data and mixed literacy .

Goals
1. A patient can get in, see their day and act on it in under 30 seconds.
2. Logging a reading or a dose takes two taps from anywhere, without leaving the screen
they are on.
3. Booking is honest: a saved place in line, never a promised hour , and a ticket that works
offline.
4. Help is one tap away on every screen.
5. One design language across all screens, in light and dark.
Non-goals for v1: real clinical data on mobile before Gate CG, real Mobile Money charges,
push delivery , voice input, video calls, USSD , wearables, and the clinician app.

Users and principles
Primary user: an adult living with diabetes and/or high blood pressure, registered at a
f acility such as Kawempe Health Centre IV . Often over 45, on a low-end Android phone,
with intermittent data, reading English or Luganda.
Secondary users: a f amily member who manages a parent's care ("People I care for"), and
the care team, who see what the patient logs.

Shape: cards 20 to 24 radius, pills 999, sheets 32 at the top. The cascade overlaps by

curve shares one centre: radius-36 ends, a radius-28 active pill with an even 8 pt inset,
and a cradle that is a true circle around the plus.
