# Diagnosis associations — CSO review (MS-EMR-DX-ASSOC-001 v1.0.0-draft)

**Status:** draft — awaiting CSO signature. Generated 2026-10-08. Data: `data/clerking/dx-associations.v1.json`.

Step 09 Impression will show, under each diagnosis on the visit, its **complications** and the conditions that **go with it**. Never alternatives. This draft types every pair already in the catalogue's untyped `related` lists. Nothing new was added.

## Method

Every pair in the clerking diagnosis catalogue's untyped `related` lists (MS-CLERK-DX v1) was typed by hand, by clinical judgement and each profile's note, into exactly one of three buckets, read in the parent-to-child direction. COMPLICATION: the child is a recognised consequence of the parent (diabetes → retinopathy; pneumonia → sepsis). GOES_WITH: the child commonly co-exists with the parent and finding the parent should prompt checking for or managing the child: shared-risk comorbidity (diabetes → hypertension), co-infection (HIV → TB), a chronic driver that needs its own ongoing care (heart failure → hypertension; AF → rheumatic heart disease; stroke → AF), and, only where the parent is the acute decompensation of a known disease, the precipitant that must be sought (DKA → sepsis). EXCLUDED: alternatives (differentials; another type, form, stage or umbrella term; the candidate causes of a not-yet-explained symptom-level entry, which are all excluded), the underlying condition of a complication when the parent already implies it (DKA → type 1 diabetes), acute or mutually exclusive candidate causes that are the parent's aetiology work-up (AKI → sepsis), not-yet-explained placeholders the parent replaces, and anything genuinely ambiguous. No pair outside `related` was added and no id outside `profiles` is used. Draft by Claude for the Clinical Safety Officer; not for use until signed.

Exclusion codes used in the appendix: **ddx** differential diagnosis · **form** another type, form, stage or umbrella term · **cause of symptom** a candidate cause under a not-yet-explained (symptom-level) entry · **placeholder** a not-yet-explained term this diagnosis replaces · **underlying** the condition a complication already implies · **aetiology** a candidate cause of the parent (its work-up, not an association) · **ambiguous** (with the reason).
