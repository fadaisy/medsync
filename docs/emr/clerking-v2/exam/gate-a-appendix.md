## J. The intimate gate (`gates.intimate`)

- **Reason for the examination** (one_or_text)
- **Consent** (one): Verbal consent given / Unable to consent: best-interests decision recorded
- **Chaperone** (one): Offered and present / Offered and declined / Not offered: emergency; "Offered and present" asks name and role

- Items stay locked until consent and chaperone are recorded.
- The gate record is written into the note ("Chaperone: Sr N. Akello, nurse. Verbal consent.").
- Every gate completion is audited with the actor and time.
- A "Declined" consent stops the examination and offers "Not examined: patient declined".

Sources: GMC-INTIMATE, UMDPC-ETHICS.
