# ADR 0267 — Substitution, and no conversion

**Status:** Accepted — 2026-10-12 (owner, also the CSO), Pharmacy Phase 1 GATE A ([gate-a-proposal](../pharmacy/phase-1/gate-a-proposal.md) §14), SU-8, CT-4, D-PH1-14, W4.

## Decision

1. A different pack of the same strength may be supplied by anyone who may dispense.
2. A different strength needs a pharmacist, a coded reason and their own typed line; it is offered only under a signed
   substitution rule and a signed mapping between strengths. The catalogue holds no strength, so until such a mapping
   is signed no strength change is offered (W4).
3. Nothing is converted between units, packs or strengths; strength is compared as text only.

## Consequences

- The pharmacist's line prints on the label marked "From the pharmacist".

## Alternatives considered

- Strength conversion: rejected (E-PH1-05).
