# ADR 0208 — The diabetes pack replaces the v0.1.0 engine on the NCD clerkship, behind a flag

**Status:** Built, off — 2026-10-05. Owner-set flag `NCD_DM_PACK_LIVE=1` (with `NCD_CLERKSHIP_LIVE=1`). Clinical use
also needs the four-eyes second reviewer (D-NCD-17); the panel says so until it is signed.

## Context

The pack (ADR 0204) ran in shadow beside the live NCD clerkship, whose form and verdicts come from the v0.1.0
catalog. The two differ in 94+ features, option codes, units, age bands and evaluator semantics (Phase 0), so the
pack cannot simply be loaded into the live catalog.

## Decision

1. **The form is the live form plus the pack's own questions.** `data/ncd/diabetes-pack-capture.json` (generated by
   `scripts/ncd/gen-pack-capture.ts` from the pack's catalog) adds 8 sections, 113 fields and 3 capabilities; the
   combined catalog keeps the live id, so a draft carries across the switch, and passes the live validator. Age,
   care setting and "could be pregnant" come from the encounter, never the form (P0-01, P0-02).
2. **One translator** (`lib/ncd/pack/live.ts`) maps the live answers into the pack's shape: same codes, renamed
   codes, reshaped options and units (the Phase 0 feature map). A broader live field maps only where reading it
   broadly errs safe; "missed treatment" counts as "insulin omitted" only when insulin is on the list. Shadow mode
   uses the same translator, so its log is what go-live would show.
3. **The browser runs the pack.** The engine is pure; the page sends a lean copy of the pack (no citations or notes,
   about 420 KB) and the decision panel recomputes as the clinician types. A test proves the lean copy gives the full
   pack's result on every synthetic patient.
4. **A decision panel of its own** (`ncd-pack-view.tsx`, model in `lib/ncd/pack/view.ts`): the one interrupt with
   every folded action, today's items, medicines to review (with the under-16 referral wording), the diagnosis by
   criteria set (default first), validity caveats, targets and bands, the care plan, and the questions that would
   complete it. The examination differential and the completeness meter stay as they were. Red is the interrupt only.
5. **The pack carries the live-surface rulings:** P0-06 (metformin with eGFR below 30 amber), P0-14 (a low band is
   toned 'low', never 'Diabetes range'); P0-01/02/03 in the translator.

## Consequences

- Flag off, nothing changes (tested).
- Turning it on is an environment change by the owner; going back is the same change.
- The clinical save into facts (cfc-map) still reads the live codes; the pack's added fields stay in the draft and
  the note until they get CFC concepts of their own.
