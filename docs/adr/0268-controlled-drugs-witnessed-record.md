# ADR 0268 — Controlled drugs: the witnessed record beside the statutory paper

**Status:** Accepted — 2026-10-12 (owner, also the CSO), Pharmacy Phase 1 GATE A ([gate-a-proposal](../pharmacy/phase-1/gate-a-proposal.md) §14), Law 9, CD-1 to CD-8, D-PH1-26 to D-PH1-28 (provisional until legal questions 2 to 4).

## Decision

1. A controlled supply needs a pharmacist's acceptance of every line, a separate witness authenticated with their own
   factor (ADR 0279), a count of each batch committed before MedSync's figure is shown, both paper attestations and
   the witness's agreement. It records the whole hand-over in one transaction.
2. A difference is recorded with both figures and both people, listed for the in-charge, and corrected by a witnessed
   movement; the supply proceeds from the counted figure.
3. A controlled line is never in the standard lane, never bought outside, never on the slip, never supplied without a
   pharmacist.
4. MedSync never says its record replaces the signed prescription or the statutory books; the register slip carries
   the LB-7 heading that says it is not the register.

## Consequences

- With no signed controlled class a flagged medicine takes the full path with no class named.

## Alternatives considered

- Replacing the paper register: rejected (Law 9; legal question 2).
