# ADR 0424 — The patient web's places sit at the foot, the page centred

**Status:** Accepted — 2026-10-26 (owner). Supersedes D-PW-02's left rail at ≥ 761 px.

## Decision

The patient web shows its places (Home, Visits, the plus, Care, Me) as the phone's floating pill at the foot
of the screen at every width, centred and at most 560 px wide. The page above it is centred, up to 1240 px.
The rail's MedSync mark and hospital initials go: the hospital is named on Me.

## Why

The owner asked for it after using the web: on a wide screen the left rail pushed the page to one side. One
pill at every width also matches the phone.
