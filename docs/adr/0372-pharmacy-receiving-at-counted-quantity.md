# ADR 0372 — Receiving at the counted quantity

**Status:** Accepted — 2026-10-21, Pharmacy Phase 2 GATE A signed by Fadhul (CSO) (`docs/pharmacy/phase-2/gate-a-proposal.md`).

Rules: RG-1 to RG-6, 5.2.

## Decision

- A delivery enters stock at the counted quantity only. The delivery note's figure stays on the discrepancy report (HMIS 087 layout, printed and never sent).
- Rejected stock kept on site is received straight into quarantine. Stock sent back writes no stock. Expired on arrival never enters stock. A short-dated line needs a recorded decision.
