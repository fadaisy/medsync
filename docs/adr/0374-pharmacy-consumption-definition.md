# ADR 0374 — The consumption definition

**Status:** Accepted — 2026-10-21, Pharmacy Phase 2 GATE A signed by Fadhul (CSO) (`docs/pharmacy/phase-2/gate-a-proposal.md`).

Rules: CN-1 to CN-5, D-PH2-08, D-PH2-11.

## Decision

- Hospital consumption is: supplied net of reversals, plus ward and cart use, plus receipts at issue-only wards.
- Internal transfers, quarantine moves and loans are not consumption. Write-offs, losses and count corrections are reported as losses.
- A day out of stock follows the signed day rule, across store and dispensing points.
