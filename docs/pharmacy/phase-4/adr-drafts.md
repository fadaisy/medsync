# Pharmacy Phase 4: ADR drafts (numbers 0434–0449 claimed; first drafted as 0425–0433)

Drafts only. Each becomes `docs/adr/NNNN-title.md` at the step that builds it, once GATE A is signed.

| ADR  | Title                                                                         | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Step |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---- |
| 0434 | A repeat is the prescriber's record beside the line                           | Authorisations are append-only rows linked to the line version, written only by a prescribing action; `ended` is a projection; no column on `prescriptions`; printed under the line in signed wording (s.37(5)) | 2–4  |
| 0435 | Renewal requests in the doctor's workspace                                    | A `renewals` lane with its own flow source and no encounter; answered by a clinician who may prescribe; never closes by itself                                                                                  | 4    |
| 0436 | Holding prepared refills                                                      | A `prepared` location kind and paired set-aside and release movements; held stock is not available; moved only by a person; reverses E-PH1-03 for prepared refills only                                         | 2, 5 |
| 0437 | Partner pharmacies are not hospitals                                          | `partner_pharmacies` and `partner_staff`, separate from `hospitals` and `user_roles`; supplies name a supplying hospital or a supplying partner, exactly one                                                    | 2, 6 |
| 0438 | The partner register: verification, expiry by projection, disclosed interests | Four-eyes verification; "in force" evaluated at every use; the record never claims regulator verification                                                                                                       | 6    |
| 0439 | Consent per routing, and the minimum partner read                             | One consent event per routing; `partnerLineView` returns exactly Law 6's fields, enforced by a schema test                                                                                                      | 7    |
| 0440 | Single-use routing codes and the shared supply lock                           | One code per routed line or instance; the hospital and the partner take the same lock; partner supplies write no hospital ledger movement                                                                       | 7, 8 |
| 0441 | Messages may name the hospital and the partner                                | Hospital message name and partner trading name allowed; never a medicine, quantity, clinic, prescriber or condition; partner names added to the banned-term scan of every other template                        | 9    |
| 0442 | The regulator's name across the 2026 Act's commencement                       | One signed string, switched by a recorded commencement                                                                                                                                                          | 9    |
