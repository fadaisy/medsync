## Key

**Severity (S)**

| S   | Word         | Meaning                                                                                                       |
| --- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Negligible   | No harm; a record is untidy and is put right                                                                  |
| 2   | Minor        | A record is wrong, or care is briefly delayed; no harm expected                                               |
| 3   | Moderate     | A medicine is missed, delayed or wrongly supplied with possible temporary harm; or one patient's privacy lost |
| 4   | Major        | A wrong medicine, strength or quantity is taken, or a controlled drug is lost, with harm likely               |
| 5   | Catastrophic | Death or permanent harm likely                                                                                |

**Likelihood (L)**, per live dispensing point

| L   | Word           | Meaning                  |
| --- | -------------- | ------------------------ |
| 1   | Very unlikely  | Not expected in years    |
| 2   | Unlikely       | About once a year        |
| 3   | Possible       | About once a month       |
| 4   | Likely         | About once a week        |
| 5   | Almost certain | Daily, or already so now |

**Risk = S × L.** 1–4 low · 5–9 medium · 10–16 high · 20–25 very high. "Initial" is with no control beyond what
Phase 0 already has. "Residual" assumes the control is built, its test passes and the hospital's signed sets are in
force.

**Test words.** Each proving test is written as its lane, its file under `tests/` without `.test.ts`, and its
title in quotes, verbatim, so it can be found with a search. `unit` = Vitest without a database (`tests/pharmacy/**`,
`tests/downtime/**`); `lane` = the PGlite lane (`EMR_PGLITE=1 vitest run tests/emr-db`, one connection, so races are
proven one transaction after another); `rls/pharmacy-races` = the real race test (two postgres-js connections
against dev Supabase), run by the owner. A title with `%s` runs once per table or fixture; `…` shortens a long
title. "Not yet written" or "not yet run" says so plainly. `S1`–`S96` are the scenarios of Section 9.

**Status words.**

- **Closed by test:** the control exists in code and a passing test proves it. The residual risk below still stands.
- **Partly closed:** the control exists and part of it is proven; another part is untested, unrun, or by its nature
  a person's word (wording only, a tick, a count). The status line says which part.
- **Open:** no control in code reduces the hazard, or the control sits outside this slice.

**What the open five mean in practice.**

- **19, quantity typed by hand:** nothing checks the number. Prescriptions carry no structured quantity; adding one
  is a prescribing change.
- **41, interactions:** MedSync does no interaction check. Every pharmacy screen says so, and the notice cannot be
  dismissed. That is the whole control.
- **48, an admission medicine stopped while its prescription stays active:** not fixed. Keep "Individual inpatient
  supply" off until it is (H7).
- **49, the doctor's print lists stopped lines:** MedSync refuses such a line at a live point; paper taken
  elsewhere is not protected.
