Three ways a product gets onto a delivery line

Scan the packA GS1 DataMatrix gives the pack, the batch and the expiry at once. A plain barcode gives the pack; you type the batch and expiry from the box.
Pick from the catalogueType any name: generic, another generic name, the brand or the NDA number. Then pick the pack. The strength, form, route and pack size fill themselves.
Type it, when it is not thereThe same fields, typed. The line is received at once as a local product and sent to MedSync's catalogue team, who check it and add it for everyone.

What the catalogue will not do

  • Give doses. Strength is a fact on the pack. Dosing stays with signed dose references.
  • Convert strengths. 500 mg is never 0.5 g by guess; packs convert to units only by the pack's own count.
  • Guess a barcode. An unknown barcode is linked by a person, once.
  • Hold up a patient for payment in an emergency or on a ward.
