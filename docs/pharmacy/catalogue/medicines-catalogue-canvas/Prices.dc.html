Prices show at the hand-over, on the patient's bill and, once Accounts exists, in its reports. A product with no price at a charged point shows "Price not set" at the window and is never billed as zero.

Three kinds of nothing

FreeThe point does not charge for this product. The bill shows it as free.
UGX 0Never used. A real zero is "Free".
Price not setThe line can still be supplied. The bill waits for the pharmacy in charge to set a price.
